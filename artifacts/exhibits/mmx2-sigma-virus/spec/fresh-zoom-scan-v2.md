## New readings the hi-res image resolves

1. **Crown**: angular helmet, NOT a smooth dome — flat top trapezoid panel, cornice beam,
   large centre panel, diagonal rafter struts descending outward both sides
   (`front_crown.png`). Rebuilt as extruded prism with straight edges so EdgesGeometry
   draws real facet seams.
2. **Eyes**: angular hexagonal bracket lenses with DOUBLE rims (outer edge + inner edge =
   recessed pocket rim seen face-on), lenses joined at centre by a continuous crossing;
   band w 0.434H at line y 0.52H (`front_brow-band-eyes.png`). Rebuilt as octagon profile
   with inner hole (extrude holes → double-rim).
3. **Nose**: rectangular column of two parallel verticals from the eye-bridge down to the
   mouth bar, small nose-bottom block where it meets the bar (`front_nose-column.png`).
4. **Mouth/chin**: horizontal mouth bar with offset raised segment left-of-centre; below it
   the jaw top edge; chin terminates in TWO square feet (tabs) at the outer corners plus a
   small centre arch hanging slightly lower — the W-shaped bottom notch
   (`front_mouth-chin.png`, `front_jaw-chintabs.png`). Mouth seam realized as a thin strip
   part (`mouthSeam`) rather than prose-only localFeature.
5. **Cheek lobes**: tall angular plates, vertical outer edges, double-line inner seam
   (plate-over-shell overlap), clearly separated from the eyes; span rows ~0.36–0.68
   (`front_cheek-lobe-left/right.png`).
